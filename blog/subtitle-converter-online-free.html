Why Use a Subtitle Converter?

Subtitles come in many formats. SRT files work on most media players, WebVTT is used for HTML5 video, and ASS supports advanced styling. Converting between formats lets you use subtitles across different platforms, extract text for translation, or create readable transcripts.

Supported Formats

  • SRT (SubRip): The most common subtitle format. Simple, widely supported by all media players.
  • VTT (WebVTT): Used for HTML5 video captions and online video platforms.
  • ASS (Advanced SubStation Alpha): Advanced format with styling, positioning, and animation support.

Output Options

  • TXT: Plain text — subtitle text only, no timestamps. Ideal for reading or translation.
  • Markdown: Timestamped text formatted as Markdown. Great for documentation or notes.
  • CSV: Tabular data with index, timestamp, and text columns. Perfect for spreadsheets or analysis.
  • JSON: Structured data with all metadata preserved. For programmatic use.

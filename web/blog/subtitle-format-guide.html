SRT (SubRip) Format

The most widely supported subtitle format. Every video player and platform supports SRT. Simple structure:

  • Sequence number
  • Timecode (start → end)
  • Subtitle text

Best for: universal compatibility across devices, media players, and video platforms.

VTT (WebVTT) Format

Modern HTML5 standard for web-based video. Similar to SRT but with additional features:

  • Optional styling with CSS-like cues
  • Support for vertical text, ruby annotations
  • Chapter markers and descriptions

Best for: websites using HTML5 <video> tag, online course platforms.

ASS (Advanced SubStation Alpha) Format

The most feature-rich subtitle format. Supports advanced styling and positioning:

  • Multiple font styles, colors, and sizes
  • Precise positioning on screen
  • Animated effects and karaoke
  • Drawing shapes and images

Best for: fansub groups, video creators who need precise subtitle placement and styling.
